成人引导小站Adult Support Station

重点Emphasize

图形由边、角、面和物体特征来判断。Shapes are identified by sides, corners, faces, and object features.

易混点Watch For

只凭颜色或朝向判断形状。Watch for judging by color or rotation.

引导问题Ask

  • 数量变多了还是变少了？Is the amount getting bigger or smaller?
  • 你先知道了什么？What do you know first?
  • 什么变了？什么没变？What changed? What stayed the same?

小技巧Tip

先摆一摆，再画一画，最后写答案。Build it first, draw it next, then write the answer.
