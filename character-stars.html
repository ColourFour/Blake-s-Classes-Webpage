👨‍👧 Read together · 亲子共读

Read to the moon

Tap the glowing star together and listen. When the hand disappears, let Lily choose. If a star bursts, copy the glowing answer. She’ll meet it again on her own.

Each trail adds four characters. Crowns mix characters, pictures and words. Stars she misses return sooner. Progress and review dates stay in this browser.

After the third crown, open your copy of Guess How Much I Love You. Read it together in three parts. Help with unfamiliar print whenever she asks.
