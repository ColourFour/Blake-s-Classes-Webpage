成人引导小站Adult Support Station

重点Emphasize

规律来自位置和变化，不是猜。Patterns come from position and change, not guessing.

易混点Watch For

只看最后一个，不看整串规律。Watch for looking only at the last item instead of the whole pattern.

引导问题Ask

  • 数量变多了还是变少了？Is the amount getting bigger or smaller?
  • 你先知道了什么？What do you know first?
  • 什么变了？什么没变？What changed? What stayed the same?

小技巧Tip

让孩子大声说：每次变了什么？Ask the child to say what changes each time.
