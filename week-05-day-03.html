重点帮什么What to Emphasize

鼓励孩子说出“为什么”，不追求速度。Invite a short “why” without pushing speed.

留意什么误会Misconception to Watch

容易只看符号或表面图案，忘了检查规律。Watch for guessing from the surface instead of checking the pattern.

可以这样问Guiding Questions

  • 你先知道了什么？What do you know first?
  • 什么变了？什么没变？What changed? What stayed the same?
  • 数量变多了还是变少了？Is the amount getting bigger or smaller?

今日小技巧Teaching Tip

如果卡住，先遮住文字，只看图说故事；再回到题目找关键词。If stuck, cover the words, tell the picture story first, then return to the key words.
