重点Emphasize

20以内仍然要看故事结构。Within 20, keep reading the story structure.

易混点Watch For

数变大后忘记比较不是合并。Watch for treating comparison as combining when numbers get larger.

引导问题Ask

  • 你先知道了什么？What do you know first?
  • 什么变了？什么没变？What changed? What stayed the same?
  • 数量变多了还是变少了？Is the amount getting bigger or smaller?
