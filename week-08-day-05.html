成人引导小站Adult Support Station

重点Emphasize

方向词要和身体、地图、视角连起来。Direction words connect to body, map, and viewpoint.

易混点Watch For

左右混淆，或换视角后仍按自己方向说。Watch for left/right confusion or ignoring viewpoint changes.

引导问题Ask

  • 我们是在合起来、拿走，还是比较？Are we joining, taking away, or comparing?
  • 你能用手指或小物品摆出来吗？Can you show it with your fingers or objects?
  • 这个图形/位置有什么线索？What clue do you see in the shape or position?

小技巧Tip

先摆一摆，再画一画，最后写答案。Build it first, draw it next, then write the answer.
