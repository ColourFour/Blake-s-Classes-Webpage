成人引导小站Adult Support Station

重点Emphasize

推理要按线索一步一步排除。Reasoning works by using clues one step at a time.

易混点Watch For

凭感觉选答案，不回到线索检查。Watch for guessing without checking the clues.

引导问题Ask

  • 你先知道了什么？What do you know first?
  • 什么变了？什么没变？What changed? What stayed the same?
  • 数量变多了还是变少了？Is the amount getting bigger or smaller?

小技巧Tip

每用一条线索，就做一个小标记。Make a small mark after using each clue.
