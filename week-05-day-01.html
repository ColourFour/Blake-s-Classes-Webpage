成人引导小站Adult Support Station

重点Emphasize

等式两边要一样，符号要为关系服务。Both sides of an equation must match; signs show the relationship.

易混点Watch For

把□当装饰，或随便选 + / -。Watch for treating the box as decoration or guessing the sign.

引导问题Ask

  • 数量变多了还是变少了？Is the amount getting bigger or smaller?
  • 你先知道了什么？What do you know first?
  • 什么变了？什么没变？What changed? What stayed the same?

小技巧Tip

先试一个数，再检查等式是否成立。Try a number, then check whether the equation is true.
