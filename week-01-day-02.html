成人引导小站Adult Support Station

重点Emphasize

数字要和真实数量连起来。Connect numerals to real amounts.

易混点Watch For

容易只认数字，不检查数量。Watch for naming numerals without checking the amount.

引导问题Ask

  • 你能用手指或小物品摆出来吗？Can you show it with your fingers or objects?
  • 这个图形/位置有什么线索？What clue do you see in the shape or position?
  • 我们是在合起来、拿走，还是比较？Are we joining, taking away, or comparing?

小技巧Tip

让孩子先点数，再写数字。Let the child touch-count before writing the numeral.
