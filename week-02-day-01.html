重点帮什么What to Emphasize

先让孩子看图和指出关键变化，再写答案。Let the child point to the picture and name the key change before writing.

留意什么误会Misconception to Watch

容易把“比较差多少”误当成“合起来”。Watch for treating “how many more” as “total.”

可以这样问Guiding Questions

  • 你能用手指或小物品摆出来吗？Can you show it with your fingers or objects?
  • 这个图形/位置有什么线索？What clue do you see in the shape or position?
  • 我们是在合起来、拿走，还是比较？Are we joining, taking away, or comparing?

今日小技巧Teaching Tip

如果卡住，先遮住文字，只看图说故事；再回到题目找关键词。If stuck, cover the words, tell the picture story first, then return to the key words.
