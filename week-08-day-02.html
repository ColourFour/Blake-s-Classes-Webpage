成人引导小站Adult Support Station

重点Emphasize

方向词要和身体、地图、视角连起来。Direction words connect to body, map, and viewpoint.

易混点Watch For

左右混淆，或换视角后仍按自己方向说。Watch for left/right confusion or ignoring viewpoint changes.

引导问题Ask

  • 什么变了？什么没变？What changed? What stayed the same?
  • 数量变多了还是变少了？Is the amount getting bigger or smaller?
  • 你先知道了什么？What do you know first?

小技巧Tip

让孩子用手指在图上走路线。Have the child walk the path with a finger.
