成人引导小站Adult Support Station

重点Emphasize

十位和个位是不同单位。Tens and ones are different units.

易混点Watch For

把14看成普通的1和4，而不是1个十和4个一。Watch for reading 14 as just 1 and 4 instead of 1 ten and 4 ones.

引导问题Ask

  • 数量变多了还是变少了？Is the amount getting bigger or smaller?
  • 你先知道了什么？What do you know first?
  • 什么变了？什么没变？What changed? What stayed the same?

小技巧Tip

先捆十个，再写十位个位。Bundle ten first, then write tens and ones.
